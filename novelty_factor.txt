Planned Novelty Factor — Hotel Booking Cancellation Prediction

Existing work on hotel cancellation prediction (including recent
papers using SHAP for explainability) identifies which booking
features drive cancellation risk — e.g. lead time, deposit type,
arrival month — but stops at ranking feature importance.

Our planned contribution extends this by translating SHAP's
feature-level explanations into an estimated monetary impact per
factor, rather than reporting importance alone. For example, instead
of reporting "lack of a deposit is the most important predictor of
cancellation," the model would estimate "bookings without a deposit
account for an estimated $X in projected lost revenue from
cancellations," giving hotel managers an actionable, profit-driven
basis for policy decisions (e.g. requiring deposits for
high-risk booking profiles).

This differs from the closest existing work (CatBoost + SHAP on a
private PMS dataset, August 2026) in two ways: (1) it uses the public
Kaggle Hotel Booking Demand dataset with Random Forest and XGBoost
models rather than a private dataset with CatBoost, and (2) it adds
the profit/cost-translation step, which the existing literature does
not include.
